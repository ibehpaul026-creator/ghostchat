Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use GhostChat, we collect:

  • Account information: your username, password (stored as a secure hash), bio, and profile picture
  • Content you post: text, photos, videos, and stories
  • Messages: private messages and group chat messages
  • Activity: posts you like, accounts you follow, and accounts you block

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your real name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use advertising tracking or analytics cookies

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to:

  • Provide and operate the platform
  • Show you content from people you follow
  • Deliver your messages to the right people
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and keep the platform safe

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on the server that runs this platform. Posts, messages and uploaded files are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Stories are automatically deleted after 24 hours.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete any post or content you have created at any time
  • Delete your entire account and all associated data from the Settings page
  • Set your account to private so only approved followers can see your posts
  • Block any user to prevent them from interacting with you

6. Passwords

Your password is never stored in plain text. It is protected using PBKDF2 with SHA-512 hashing and a unique salt, which means even the platform owner cannot read your password.

7. Messages

Private messages are stored on the server so they can be delivered. They are not read or shared with anyone except the intended recipients. We do not sell message content.

8. Children

This platform is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about your privacy or want to request deletion of your data, contact us through the platform's support system.
